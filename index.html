Materials

The timber behind every piece

We work with a small set of hardwoods, chosen for how they age, not just how they look on day one.

Sheesham

Rich golden-brown with a bold, flowing grain. Naturally resistant to moisture and daily wear — a dependable choice for furniture that gets used every day.

Best for dining tables, beds

Teak

Natural oils resist termites and weathering. Ages into a deeper, warmer tone over the years — built to last generations.

Best for outdoor & heirloom pieces

Mango Wood

Light and subtly grained, with real strength for its weight. A more sustainable hardwood choice, sourced from trees past their fruiting years.

Best for shelving, modern furniture

Acacia

Dense and scratch-resistant, with striking natural variation from piece to piece — no two tables look quite the same.

Best for coffee tables, cabinets
