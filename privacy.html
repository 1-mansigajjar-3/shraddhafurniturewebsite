This policy explains how Shraddha Furniture ("we", "us"), based in Mehsana, Gujarat, India, handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us.

What we collect

InformationWhen you give it
Full name and email addressYou send a message through the contact form
Phone number (optional)You add it to the contact form
Your message, including room details and style preferencesYou send a message through the contact form
Anything you share on a call, email or WhatsAppYou contact us directly

We do not ask for payment details on this website, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote or design proposal
  • To arrange measurements, delivery and installation if you go ahead with an order
  • To keep records of our work and meet our legal and tax obligations

We rely on your consent, which you give when you send the form, and on our legitimate need to respond to and fulfil your request.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. Some services help us run the website and handle your messages, and they may process limited data on our behalf:

  • EmailJS delivers your contact form message to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr load the fonts and scripts the page needs, so your browser connects to them and they receive your IP address.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook links take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. Anything you send there is handled by them under their terms.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and similar storage to keep the website working and to remember your cookie choice. We only use analytics cookies if you click Accept on our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer. Third-party services listed above may also set their own cookies or log your visit according to their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages for up to 12 months after our last contact with you. If you become a customer, we keep order records for as long as needed for warranty, accounting and legal purposes. After that we delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or complete it
  • Ask us to erase it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Raise a complaint with us, and with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response

If you live outside India, local law may give you additional rights, and we will respect them where they apply.
